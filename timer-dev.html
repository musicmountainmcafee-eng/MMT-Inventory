        Cue times

        Clock-time cues are minutes from show time (−60 is an hour before) and roll over by themselves. Timed cues start when the SM presses GO. Curtain speech and acts count up from zero; intermission counts down. Tap Colors under any cue to choose when its clock changes color.

        Stage managers

        Each stage manager signs in to the controller with their own 4-digit PIN, and that device stays signed in for 13 hours. With no PINs set, the controller doesn't ask. Pick tonight's stage manager with one tap on the controller. After the show, the report can be emailed or texted to them. Names, emails and phone numbers travel with the schedule (inside the links and over the relay), so keep the links in the building.

        Deck calls

        One per line. These are the buttons the SM taps to flash a call on the backstage screen.

        Tech week calls

        The call buttons while a tech day is running. One per line.

        Links

        Use these to set up a new device. Changes you make in Settings reach every screen on their own, so there's no need to resend links after editing.

        Download a launcher on the computer that will show the display. It already has this display's link inside. Unzip it and double-click it, then drag the window to any size. On a Mac, if it's blocked the first time, open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Needs Firefox or Chrome on a Mac (Chrome or Edge on Windows). You can also get it from the display itself: move the mouse over a full-screen display.

        Stream Deck

        Each button below is a web address. In the Stream Deck app, drag a Website action (under System) onto a key, paste the address, and tick GET request in background. The key then works from anywhere with internet, with no computer needing the timer open. These addresses are fixed, not tied to a code, so they never need to be redone.

        If the Stream Deck is plugged into the booth computer with the controller open and in front, Hotkey actions work too: Space GO, B back, H hold, + one minute, − one minute, 1 to 9 the deck calls in order, 0 or Esc clears the call.
